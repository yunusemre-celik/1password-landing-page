Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about 1Password.

Have questions about our zero-knowledge model or enterprise deployment? We have answers.

Zero-knowledge means that your data is encrypted and decrypted exclusively on your local device using keys that only you possess. 1Password’s servers only hold encrypted cipher blocks. Even if our servers were seized by government subpoena or breached by an adversary, no one can read your passwords without your Master Password and 128-bit Secret Key.
The Secret Key is a unique 128-bit code generated locally when you create your account. It works together with your Master Password to protect your data with dual-key cryptography. Unlike a simple 2FA token, the Secret Key directly participates in the encryption of your master vault key, making brute-force dictionary attacks computationally impossible.
Passkeys are built on the FIDO2 / WebAuthn standard and replace passwords with cryptographic key pairs. Your public key is registered with the website, while your private key is safely encrypted inside your 1Password vault. When you sign in, 1Password performs a biometric signature that cannot be phished or replicated.
As autonomous AI agents and automated workflows proliferate, hardcoded API keys represent an acute breach risk. 1Password Unified Access allows security teams to issue ephemeral, scoped service account tokens to AI models and CI/CD pipelines, complete with audit logging and instant revocation.
